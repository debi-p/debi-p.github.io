EdgeCore - Scientific AI Runtime

Building reusable local inference capabilities so scientific applications can use AI near instruments and customer data, with the goal of reducing cloud dependence and supporting constrained-connectivity environments.

Business Challenge

Scientific teams need AI close to laboratory instruments. Separate serving stacks create duplication, while cloud-only inference can introduce cost, connectivity, and data-handling constraints.

Architecture & Delivery Ownership

Led architecture and hands-on delivery of the reusable inference layer on NVIDIA GB10 ARM64 hardware, including container deployment, authenticated APIs, configuration troubleshooting, performance evaluation, and team runbooks.

Validated Outcomes

  • Deployed GPT-OSS-20B chat and BGE-M3 embedding services using vLLM, LiteLLM, Docker, and Komodo.
  • Integrated authenticated OpenAI-compatible chat, streaming, and embedding APIs; validated external-client access through SSH tunneling.
  • Reduced measured chat-runtime memory allocation from 83.69 GiB to 29.08 GiB (about 65%) by tuning the runtime memory budget and KV-cache allocation.
  • Resolved serving failures involving authentication, environment configuration, and numeric configuration types to restore successful API operation.
  • Evaluated request latency, token usage, concurrent execution, and admission control using client measurements and vLLM metrics.
  • Documented deployment procedures, resource measurements, configuration decisions, and validation limitations for repeatable team use.

Stage: local AI services validated on GB10. Instrument integration, broader platform capabilities, and air-gapped operation remain future objectives. The 65% figure describes measured chat-runtime memory allocation, not overall infrastructure cost savings.

PyLens - Evidence Synthesis & Adjudication

Transforms scientific articles into evidence-linked model inputs using specialized expert models, independent judges, and conflict adjudication, with human review and traceable decisions throughout.

Business Challenge

Extracting population definitions, model structures, costs, and utility values from articles and supplementary tables is time-consuming. Unsupported claims, inconsistent units, and conflicting findings make automated extraction difficult to trust.

Engineering Approach

Separated document extraction, domain summarization, questionnaire generation, independent verification, and expert revisions into an orchestrated workflow. Source references and confidence annotations keep findings connected to the supporting evidence.

Core Outcomes

  • Scientific article synthesis into categorized summaries and model-ready questionnaires.
  • Evidence-linked model inputs with source references, units, and confidence annotations.
  • Reconciliation of conflicting answers, with unresolved findings available for expert review.
  • Interactive revisions with persisted versions, supporting rationale, and traceable model calls.

Publication to Reviewed Model Inputs

  1. Document IntakeArticles, supplementary material, text, tables, and figures
  2. Extraction & SynthesisTable structuring and parallel domain summaries
  3. VerificationEvidence checks, unit normalization, and reconciliation
  4. Expert ReviewQuestionnaires, interactive revisions, and finalization

Specialized Model Roles

Generation & Reasoning
High-capability models handle table interpretation, domain synthesis, and complex questionnaire responses.
Independent Verification
A separate verifier checks claims against source excerpts, flags unsupported findings, and reconciles inconsistencies.
Routing & Interaction
Lighter models classify chat, revision, and finalization requests to balance responsiveness and inference cost.
Workflow & State
LangGraph coordinates processing stages; a FastAPI backend manages background runs, session state, persistence, and application access.

Technology Ecosystem

Python · LangGraph · FastAPI · Angular · OpenAI · Anthropic · Google Gemini · Document Extraction · JSON Schema Validation · LLM Tracing

Scientific Synthesis

  • Extracts article text, table content, figures, and document metadata.
  • Produces parallel summaries across population, costs, utilities, decision trees, and state-transition models.
  • Maps extracted findings into typed questionnaire responses with supporting evidence.

Review & Reliability

  • Checks source support, numeric consistency, units, and confidence.
  • Combines questionnaire sections and reconciles conflicting responses.
  • Supports expert chat, targeted revisions, version history, and final review.

Operational Design

Background processing acknowledges uploads promptly while synthesis continues. Session tracking, retries, provider fallbacks, prompt versioning, and model-call tracing support diagnosis and reproducibility.

Cost & Quality

Assigns demanding reasoning and verification tasks to capable models while routing simpler interactions to lighter models. Evidence checks and expert review support trustworthy use; they do not eliminate the need for scientific judgment.

AnalyticalGraph - AI-Powered Scientific Data Platform

Unifies scientific data storage, knowledge graphs, and LLM-powered discovery across mass spectrometry, structural biology, and genomics.

Scientific Challenge

Instrument results contain valuable molecular and experimental information, but file boundaries and domain-specific formats make relationships difficult to explore. Researchers need searchable scientific context alongside the underlying analytical data.

Platform Approach

Transforms scientific files into structured entities and graph relationships, preserves analytical traces, and exposes an AI-assisted query layer so researchers can discover data through natural-language questions.

Key Capabilities

  • Scientific knowledge graphs connect compounds, peaks, spectra, samples, studies, instruments, and experimental context.
  • Natural-language query processing combines semantic matching, named-entity recognition, and model-assisted interpretation with SPARQL-based graph retrieval.
  • Mass spectrometry exploration brings graph relationships together with spectrum and chromatogram traces.
  • Reusable ingestion processors support Compound Discoverer, CIF structure, and VCF variant data.

Cloud Optimization - AWS & Microservices

Evidence-led rationalisation of a scientific platform ecosystem spanning more than 100 microservices, connecting technical telemetry with business value and lifecycle decisions.

Platform Challenge

A long-lived platform accumulates overlapping capabilities and operational overhead. Low adoption can coexist with continuing infrastructure cost, while missing telemetry can make active services appear unused.

Engineering Approach

Assess service ownership, production usage, AWS spend, operational health, dependencies, and customer impact together. Translate the evidence into retain, optimize, consolidate, modernize, or retirement recommendations.

Decision Value

  • Identifies capacity and architectural simplification opportunities without treating missing traffic as proof of no usage.
  • Separates low-volume but business-critical services from genuinely redundant capabilities.
  • Connects proposed changes to dependency checks, product-owner review, migration needs, and operational validation.

The reference records analysis and recommendations with mixed implementation status. Proposed capacity changes, retirements, and estimated savings are not presented as completed outcomes. The image is a conceptual visualization, not an actual dashboard screenshot.

Evidence to Lifecycle Decision

  1. InventoryServices, capabilities, ownership, and business domains
  2. Correlate EvidenceUsage, cost, health, vulnerabilities, and dependencies
  3. Review OptionsRetain, right-size, consolidate, modernize, or retire
  4. Validate ChangeConsumer confirmation, approval, and post-change monitoring

Assessment Dimensions

Adoption & Usage
Requests, service invocations, active users, and adoption trends; API telemetry is distinguished from asynchronous and background workloads.
Cloud Footprint
AWS compute, storage, database, networking, and managed-service cost inform capacity and cost reviews.
Operational Risk
Incidents, SLA commitments, vulnerabilities, deployment activity, and technical debt inform change risk.
Dependency & Business Context
Application consumers, service relationships, business criticality, customer impact, and migration effort guide safe disposition.

Optimization Opportunities

  • Pod-capacity rightsizing based on observed demand and availability needs.
  • Duplicate capability and service consolidation assessment.
  • Modernization options, including managed-service alternatives.
  • Retirement candidates supported by verified consumer and business evidence.

Change Safeguards

  • Do not equate absent metrics with absent consumers.
  • Validate peak behavior, latency, errors, and saturation before reducing capacity.
  • Confirm consumers and migrate routes before removing dependencies.
  • Obtain business approval and observe behavior after cutover.

Financial Discipline

Estimated savings support prioritization, but remain separate from realized savings until approved changes are implemented and verified. No aggregate savings total is claimed from the partial recommendation tables.
